About Ghana Drive

Redefining Driver Education In Ghana

Ghana Drive was established with a singular mission: to make Ghanaian roads safer by developing highly skilled, defensive, and responsible drivers.

Located on Obuom Road in Kasoa, our ultra-modern facility incorporates classroom theoretical instruction, computer-based DVLA mock test simulators, and dual-control vehicle fleet training.

Our Vision

To become West Africa's leading technology-driven driver training institution.

Our Mission

Zero road accidents through structured defensive driving education.
