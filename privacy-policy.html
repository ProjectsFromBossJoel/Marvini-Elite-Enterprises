1. Introduction

Marvini Elite Enterprises ("we," "us," or "our"), headquartered in Accra, Ghana, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your information across our website and group subsidiaries, including:

M-Smart Driving School Solution M-Digital Food Chain M-Events & Festivals M-Farms M-Consultancy & Training

2. Information We Collect

  • Personal Data: Full name, email address, phone number, and organization details submitted via contact forms, newsletter subscriptions, or program registrations.
  • Usage Data: Technical data including IP address, browser type, device information, and pages visited.
  • Subsidiary-Specific Data: Records relevant to specific services (e.g., driving school registration details or training enrolment forms).

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To process inquiries, registrations, and newsletter subscriptions.
  • To deliver and improve enterprise services across our subsidiaries.
  • To comply with statutory and regulatory obligations (e.g., DVLA requirements for driving school services).
  • To send updates, newsletters, and administrative notices.

4. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We may share information with:

  • Group Subsidiaries: To fulfil multi-disciplinary requests or specific service offerings.
  • Regulatory & Government Bodies: When required by law or official regulatory frameworks (e.g., DVLA, MoFA).
  • Service Providers: Trusted third-party vendors operating under strict confidentiality standards to maintain website operations and service delivery.

5. Data Security & Retention

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to safeguard your data against unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure. Personal information is retained only as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes outlined in this policy or comply with legal obligations.

6. Your Rights

Depending on applicable data protection laws (including Ghana's Data Protection Act), you have the right to:

  • Access, update, or correct your personal data.
  • Request deletion of your personal records.
  • Opt-out of promotional communications at any time.

7. Contact Us

For privacy-related inquiries or to exercise your rights, contact us at marvinieliteenterprise@gmail.com or write to our headquarters in Accra, Ghana.
